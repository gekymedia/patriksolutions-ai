# Module 3 — Getting Started With AI Assistants

**Estimated time:** ~60 minutes · **Lessons:** 4

## Learning objectives
By the end of this module you will be able to:
- Choose an AI assistant that fits you and create a free account.
- Find your way around the chat screen (the few buttons that matter).
- Start a conversation, ask follow-ups, and start a fresh chat when you should.
- Use attachments and voice where available, and keep your chats organized.

## Lesson 3.2 — The chat screen: the 5 things that matter

Every one of these tools looks slightly different and they redesign often — so instead of memorizing buttons, learn the **five things** every one of them has:

1. **The message box** (bottom of the screen). You type here and press Enter (or the send arrow).
2. **The conversation area** (middle). Your messages and the AI's replies stack here like a text thread.
3. **"New chat" button** (usually top-left or a pencil/plus icon). Starts a fresh, empty conversation.
4. **Chat history** (a side list). Your past conversations, so you can reopen them.
5. **Attach / upload** (a paperclip or "+" near the message box, where available). Lets you add a file, photo, or screenshot for the AI to read.

That's genuinely the whole interface. If you can find those five, you can use any AI assistant — today's version or next year's.

**One setting worth checking:** most tools have a **privacy / data controls** setting where you can turn off "use my chats to train the model." We'll cover why in Module 5 — just know it's there.

## Lesson 3.3 — Your first real conversation (and when to start a new chat)

Let's make the first chat count.

**Start with context, not just a question.** Instead of `write a caption`, try: `I run a small home-care business. Write a warm Facebook caption announcing we now offer weekend visits.` The second one gets a far better answer because the AI knows who you are and what you want. (This is the whole idea of Module 4 — you're getting a head start.)

**Then have a conversation.** You don't have to get it perfect in one message. React and refine:
- `Make it shorter.`
- `Add a friendly question at the end.`
- `Give me two more versions with different tones.`

The AI remembers everything *in the current chat*, so each follow-up builds on the last. This back-and-forth is where the good results come from.

**When to start a NEW chat:** start fresh when you switch to a **different, unrelated task**. Why? Because the AI carries the whole conversation as context — if your chat is half about a birthday poem and you suddenly ask about taxes, the earlier stuff can muddy the answer. One task → one chat. It also keeps your history tidy.

**When to stay in the same chat:** keep going when you're still working on the *same* thing — refining, expanding, translating, or asking follow-ups about it.

## Lesson 3.4 — Attachments, voice, and staying organized

Modern assistants can do more than plain text (availability varies by tool and plan — all have *some* of this on free tiers):

- **Attach a document or photo.** Upload a PDF, a photo of a letter, or a screenshot and ask: `Summarize this` or `What does this form want me to do?` Hugely useful for real life.
- **Voice input.** Tap the microphone and talk instead of typing — great on your phone.
- **Rename and pin chats.** Give a chat a clear name ("Meal plans") so you can find it later. Some tools let you pin or folder them.
- **Copy & regenerate.** There's usually a copy button on each answer, and a "regenerate / try again" option if you want a different take.

**A healthy habit:** one clearly-named chat per ongoing topic. "Weekly meal plans," "Work emails," "Trip to Accra." Future-you will thank present-you.

## 🎬 Instructor demonstration script

1. **Create the account live** (or show a pre-made one). Point to Sign up → Continue with Google → done. "Under two minutes, free."
2. **Point to the five things** on screen: message box, conversation area, New chat, history, attach. "Find these five on any AI tool, ever."
3. **First real prompt with context:** `I'm a nurse studying for an exam. Explain how blood pressure is measured, in simple terms, in about 150 words.` Show the quality.
4. **Refine with follow-ups:** `Make it simpler.` then `Now give me 3 flashcard questions on it.` Show that it builds on the thread.
5. **Attach something:** upload a photo of a paragraph or a short PDF and ask `Summarize this in 3 bullets.` "This is where it gets powerful for real tasks."
6. **Start a NEW chat** for an unrelated question to model the "one task, one chat" rule.
7. **Open data controls** and show the "don't train on my chats" toggle. "We'll explain this in Module 5 — just know where it lives."

## 🛠️ Hands-on exercise (20 min)
1. Create (or open) your free AI account.
2. Find and name the **five** screen elements out loud or in your notes.
3. Start a chat with **context** about yourself and ask for something real you'd actually use.
4. Improve the answer with **at least two follow-ups** in the same chat.
5. **Attach** a photo or document (a letter, a form, an article) and ask the AI to summarize it. (If your tool/plan can't, type or paste the text instead.)
6. Start a **new chat** for a totally different question.

**You're done when** you've refined one answer through follow-ups, read one document via attach-or-paste, and correctly started a fresh chat for a new task.

## ✅ Quiz (with answers & explanations)

**Q1. How much does it cost to do this course's tasks with an AI assistant?**
A) $20/month minimum
B) Nothing — the free plans are enough
C) It's included in membership
D) You must buy all four tools
**Answer: B.** Free tiers of ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini/Copilot are enough. (They're third-party tools, not included in your Patrik Solutions AI membership.)

**Q2. You just finished planning a birthday party and now want help with your taxes. You should…**
A) Keep going in the same chat
B) Start a new chat
C) Delete the app
D) Switch to a different tool
**Answer: B.** New, unrelated task → new chat, so old context doesn't muddy the answer.

**Q3. Which of these is NOT one of the five things every AI chat screen has?**
A) A message box
B) A "New chat" button
C) A built-in printer
D) Chat history
**Answer: C.** Message box, conversation area, New chat, history, and attach are the five. No printer.

**Q4. Why start your first message with context about who you are and what you want?**
A) It's polite
B) The AI gives a much better, more relevant answer
C) It makes the AI faster
D) It's required or it won't respond
**Answer: B.** Context dramatically improves relevance — the core skill of Module 4.

**Q5. True or false: You must get the perfect result in your very first message.**
**Answer: False.** The back-and-forth (follow-ups) is where great results come from.
