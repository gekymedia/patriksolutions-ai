The good news for anyone starting out is that the most powerful AI tools have generous free tiers — you can learn and do real work without spending a cedi, a dollar, or a pound. The trick is not collecting every tool, but learning one well before adding the next.

## Start with a chat assistant

A chat assistant is your foundation. It answers questions, writes, explains, brainstorms, and plans. Learn one of these well and you are already most of the way there:

- **ChatGPT** — a great all-rounder for writing, ideas, and learning.
- **Claude** — strong at longer writing, careful reasoning, and working with documents.
- **Google Gemini** — useful and well connected to Google Search and Google tools.

Pick one, use it every day for a week, and you will quickly see what it can do.

## Tools for writing and productivity

Once you are comfortable with a chat assistant, these help you get everyday work done:

- **Microsoft Copilot** — helpful if you already use Word, Excel, and PowerPoint.
- **A free grammar and spelling checker** — polishes anything you write.
- **Your chat assistant** — doubles as an editor, summariser, and idea generator.

For most beginners, a chat assistant plus one office tool covers the majority of daily tasks.

## Tools for images and design

When you need visuals — flyers, social posts, simple logos — these free options are a strong start:

- **Canva** — templates plus built-in AI features for design, no skills needed.
- **Bing Image Creator** — generates images from a text description.

You do not need to be a designer. Describe what you want clearly and adjust from there.

## How to choose and get started

1. **Begin with one chat assistant.** Do not open five tabs on day one.
2. **Use it on real tasks** — a message you need to write, a topic you want explained, a plan you need to make.
3. **Learn to prompt well.** Tell the tool who it should act as, what you want, any context, and the format. Clear instructions give far better results.
4. **Add a second tool only when you hit a specific need** — a design job, an office task, an image.

### Tips for beginners
- **Free tiers are enough to start.** Do not pay for anything until you know you need it.
- **Always check the output.** AI can be confident and still wrong — verify facts and numbers.
- **Keep your prompts** that work well so you can reuse them.
- **Protect your information.** Do not paste passwords or sensitive personal data into any tool.

### Common mistakes to avoid
- Jumping between tools instead of mastering one.
- Expecting perfect results from a vague prompt.
- Paying for subscriptions before you understand the free versions.
- Copying output without reading and improving it.
